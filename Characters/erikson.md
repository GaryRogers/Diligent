# Lieutenant Michael Erickson
## Chief Engineer, USS Diligent

### Basic Information
- **Species**: Human
- **Age**: 26-28
- **Rank**: Lieutenant (first major leadership role)
- **Position**: Chief Engineer
- **Homeworld**: Alpha Centauri
- **Previous Assignment**: Galaxy-class starship (not Enterprise)

### Background
- Raised on Alpha Centauri in the heart of Federation space
- Grew up with idealistic view of Starfleet as heroes and explorers
- Excelled in Starfleet Academy engineering program - strong theoretical knowledge
- Previously served on a Galaxy-class starship as part of large engineering department
- Recently promoted to Chief Engineer and transferred to USS Diligent
- First independent command of an engineering department

### The Adjustment Challenge
**From Galaxy-Class to Diligent**:
- **Then**: One of hundreds of engineers, could specialize, extensive backup and redundancy
- **Now**: Leading 8-12 engineers (mostly enlisted), responsible for everything
- **Then**: State-of-the-art systems, everything by the book, rarely pushed to limits
- **Now**: Older ship, jury-rigged solutions, constantly operating at extremes
- **Then**: Routine patrols and exploration missions
- **Now**: Racing to emergencies, operating in hazardous conditions, systems constantly stressed

### Personality & Traits
- **Technically brilliant**: Exceptional understanding of theory and complex systems
- **Management inexperience**: Still learning to delegate and make command decisions under pressure
- **Over-explainer**: When stressed, launches into detailed technical explanations that others don't need or want
- **Idealistic**: Genuinely believes in Starfleet's mission, struggles when reality doesn't match ideals
- **Imposter syndrome**: Worries he was transferred because he failed somehow, questions if he's ready
- **Earnest**: Takes his responsibilities seriously, sometimes too seriously
- **Perfectionistic**: Wants everything done "right" even when "good enough quickly" would save lives

### Engineering Style
- Prefers elegant, by-the-book solutions
- Learning to embrace improvisation and "make it work" approaches
- Struggles with accepting calculated risks to ship systems
- Growing more comfortable with creative problem-solving under pressure
- Tends to want to fix problems himself rather than delegate

### Key Relationships
**With V'Lara**: 
- She terrifies him initially
- Her demands seem impossible ("I need emergency power to transporters NOW")
- He tries to explain technical limitations; she just stares until he figures it out
- Gradually earns her respect by consistently delivering under pressure
- Learning that she trusts competence, not excuses

**With enlisted engineering crew**:
- Still learning to be their chief, not their peer
- They respect his knowledge but sometimes frustrated by his hesitation
- Needs to learn when to lead vs. when to get his hands dirty

### Character Arc Potential
- Learning that managing a small ship's engineering department during critical SAR missions is MORE challenging and impressive than being a specialist on a Galaxy-class
- Developing confidence in his leadership abilities
- Embracing improvisation and "good enough" solutions
- Understanding that failure is part of SAR work
- Growing from anxious junior officer to confident department head

### Character Notes
- Name reflects Alpha Centauri's human colonial heritage (Scandinavian/Northern European influence)
- Represents the "new generation" of post-Dominion War officers
- His growth mirrors the ship's mission: doing extraordinary things with limited resources
