# Lt. (j.g.) Rachel "Sec" Harlow
## Chief of Security/Tactical, USS Diligent

### Basic Information
- **Species**: Human
- **Age**: 24-25
- **Rank**: Lieutenant (junior grade) - very junior for a department head
- **Position**: Chief of Security/Tactical
- **Homeworld**: Tiny, obscure Federation colony ("You've never heard of it")
- **Time on Diligent**: 6-9 months
- **Preferred name**: "Sec" - thinks it sounds tough and experienced

### Background
**Early Life**:
- Grew up on a remote, backwater Federation colony
- Never saw real Starfleet operations until the Academy
- Colony was threatened during Dominion War but never attacked
- Idealized view of Starfleet from news reports and wartime propaganda

**The Dominion War Complex**:
- Was 17-19 years old during the war's end (2375)
- Desperately wanted to enlist and fight
- Too young - had to watch from her colony as others became heroes
- Romanticizes the war as the defining moment she missed
- Joined Starfleet immediately after, driven by "catching up"
- Doesn't understand the trauma actual war veterans carry
- Hero-worships combat veterans and war heroes

**Career Path**:
- Joined Starfleet at earliest opportunity
- Excelled at Academy in tactical training
- First posting details TBD - likely had issues with judgment/aggression
- Transferred to Diligent 6-9 months ago
- **Believes she earned this posting through merit**
- Does NOT know she's part of Admiral Seron's unofficial training program

### The Hidden Truth
**Admiral Seron's Project**:
- Vulcan Admiral, V'Lara's adoptive uncle
- Quietly identifies talented but rough junior officers
- Places them on Diligent for "seasoning" without their knowledge
- Almost all emerge as excellent officers who excel in Starfleet
- Harlow is the latest in a long line of these "projects"

**Who Knows**:
- **Admiral Seron**: Arranged the transfer
- **V'Lara**: Knows and is mildly annoyed by her uncle's continued interference
- **The Captain**: Was briefed when they took command
- **Doc Naveem**: Recognizes the pattern immediately, has seen multiple cycles
- **Harlow**: Completely unaware - thinks she earned this position

**When the Truth Comes Out** (inevitable story beat):
- Will feel humiliated and betrayed
- "Everyone knew but me?"
- Anger at Seron, V'Lara, the Captain
- Will likely double down on proving herself (potentially dangerous)
- Trust issues with command structure

### Personality & Issues
**Core Problems**:
- **Overcompensates constantly**: Tries to be the "hardass" to seem experienced
- **Poor threat assessment**: Either overreacts to minor issues or underestimates real danger
- **Doesn't respect SAR work initially**: "We're Starfleet, not the coast guard"
- **Sees threats everywhere**: Runs excessive security drills, overprepares for combat scenarios
- **Chip on shoulder about age**: Defensive when reminded of inexperience - "Don't call me 'kid'"
- **Romanticizes combat**: Thinks courage equals willingness to fight
- **Limited real-world experience**: Academy knowledge without practical wisdom

**Driving Need**:
- Desperate to prove she's a "real" Starfleet officer
- Wants to be taken seriously by veterans
- Fears being seen as incompetent or a charity case
- Measures herself against imagined war heroes

### Command Style
- **By-the-book militant**: Treats every situation with military protocol
- **Overly aggressive**: Recommends tactical solutions when diplomacy would work
- **Struggles with informal culture**: Diligent's casualness feels unprofessional to her
- **Poor delegation**: Tries to handle everything herself to prove competence
- **Defensive about decisions**: Takes criticism as personal attacks

### The "Sec" Nickname
- Insists everyone call her "Sec" instead of "Lieutenant" or "Rachel"
- Thinks it makes her sound tough and experienced
- Senior enlisted probably humor her with mild amusement
- V'Lara deliberately calls her "Lieutenant" as a distancing choice
- The nickname is a shield against vulnerability

### Key Relationships
**With V'Lara**:
- Desperately wants V'Lara's approval and respect
- V'Lara maintains cold professionalism, barely tolerates her
- "Secure the perimeter, Lieutenant." - curt, direct orders
- V'Lara doesn't coddle her but also doesn't explain herself
- Harlow doesn't know V'Lara is protecting her from repeating past tragedy
- V'Lara sees echoes of the officer who died in Harlow's behavior

**With Doc Naveem**:
- Doc recognizes the "Seron pattern" immediately
- Most patient with her among senior staff
- Sees her youth and overcompensation clearly
- Tries to mentor subtly without being obvious
- Probably warns V'Lara: "She reminds me of [deceased officer]. Don't let this one get the same idea about being a hero."
- Harlow might open up to him more than others

**With the Captain**:
- Captain knows the truth about her assignment
- Has to balance her development with crew safety
- Can't easily discipline or transfer her due to Admiral Seron's interest
- Awkward "parent but not parent" dynamic
- When truth comes out, their relationship will be severely strained

**With D'Greven**:
- D'Greven may see a younger version of herself (by-the-book, trying to prove worth)
- Could be mentor or could clash over "proper procedures"
- Both are somewhat outsiders to Diligent's culture
- Might commiserate about the informal atmosphere

**With Erickson**:
- Fellow young officer, but he's competent in his role
- Might commiserate about being "the kids" on a veteran crew
- Or might be frustrated by her attitude toward SAR work
- Could develop friendship or rivalry

**With Senior Enlisted**:
- They've seen "Seron projects" before
- Probably recognize what she is even if they don't say it
- Humor her while gently steering her away from bad decisions
- Respect earned slowly through competence, not demanded

### Small Security Detail
- Diligent has minimal security personnel (maybe 6-8 total?)
- Focuses on protecting away teams and rescue operations, not ship combat
- Harlow probably wishes she commanded more people
- Has to do hands-on work herself, not just delegate

### Growth Arc Potential
**What She Needs to Learn**:
- Courage in rescue work is different from combat courage
- Preventing violence is harder than responding with violence
- Real veterans carry trauma, not glory
- Her small colony background doesn't make her lesser
- Being "seasoned" by Diligent is an honor, not a punishment
- Trust is earned through competence and humility, not bravado

**Potential Turning Points**:
- First rescue mission where her tactical training actually saves lives
- Seeing Doc or V'Lara deal with mission failure
- Learning about the officer who died (when ready for that truth)
- Discovery of her "project" status - crisis point
- Earning genuine respect from crew through growth

### The Predecessor's Shadow
- There was a previous "Seron project" officer who died on mission
- Was doing well, maturing properly
- Death bonded V'Lara and Doc (origin of "V" nickname)
- V'Lara is harder on Harlow partially because of this loss
- Doc watches Harlow carefully for similar warning signs
- When Harlow learns this story, it will recontextualize everything

### Character Notes
- Name is intentionally ordinary/unremarkable - from a nobody colony
- Represents the "outside view" of what Starfleet should be vs. what it is
- Her arc is about redefining what makes a good officer
- The irony: She's on the best possible ship for her development, but can't see it yet
- Admiral Seron's program works because Diligent *is* the real Starfleet
