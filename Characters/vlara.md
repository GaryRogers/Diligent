# Lt. Commander Maren V'Lara
## Chief Rescue Officer, USS Diligent

### Basic Information
- **Species**: Orion (Vulcan-raised)
- **Age**: 48
- **Rank**: Lt. Commander (declined promotion to Commander multiple times)
- **Position**: Chief Rescue Officer - Operational commander during active rescue missions
- **Years of Service**: 30 years in Starfleet

### Background
- Adopted by Vulcan relief workers at age 4-6 after [incident TBD - Syndicate raid? Colony disaster?]
- Raised on Vulcan with traditional Vulcan education and emotional discipline training
- Joined Starfleet at age 18
- Specialized in search and rescue operations throughout career
- Has the highest successful rescue rate in her sector
- Repeatedly declined promotion to Commander - prefers field operations to command

### Personality & Traits
- **Stoic and mission-focused**: Trained in Vulcan emotional control, but not emotionless - cares deeply, shows it through decisive action
- **Direct communicator**: No patience for politics, bureaucracy, or inefficiency when lives are at stake
- **Fiercely protective**: Will go to extreme lengths to protect her team and rescue survivors
- **Uncompromising**: Measures success only in lives saved, not commendations or career advancement
- **Respected but intimidating**: Crew respects her intensity and competence; some find her difficult to approach

### Command Style
- Once rescue operations begin, she is the **operational commander** with full authority over mission execution
- Expects immediate compliance from her team during active operations
- Willing to take calculated risks with herself, more cautious with her team
- Frequently clashes with the Captain over acceptable risk levels and mission parameters
- Philosophy: "We didn't come all this way to leave them behind"
- Leads from the front - never asks her team to do anything she wouldn't do herself

### The Orion Factor
- Deliberately cultivated stoic professionalism as rejection of Orion stereotypes
- Uses pheromone suppressants and/or Vulcan-taught control techniques
- Background gives her unique insight into criminal operations (smugglers, traffickers, Syndicate tactics)
- Has forged her own identity: neither traditionally Orion nor fully Vulcan

### Key Conflict
As Chief Rescue Officer, Maren commands **rescue operations** while the Captain commands the **ship**. This creates natural tension:
- When do rescue priorities override ship safety?
- Who decides when conditions are too dangerous?
- What level of risk is acceptable to save lives?

### Character Notes
- The name contrast (soft "Maren" vs. harder "V'Lara") reflects her dual nature
- She represents subversion of Orion stereotypes through Starfleet service
- Her Vulcan upbringing informs but doesn't define her - she's chosen compassionate action over pure logic
- 30 years of experience makes her invaluable but also set in her ways
