# Lt. Commander Sarona D'Greven
## Executive Officer/Second Officer, USS Diligent

### Basic Information
- **Species**: Trill (unjoined)
- **Age**: Early 40s
- **Rank**: Lt. Commander
- **Position**: Executive Officer/Second Officer
- **Time on Diligent**: Approximately 2.5 years (halfway through first tour)
- **Background**: Sciences division, transitioned to command track upon joining Diligent

### Background
**Career Path**:
- Trained and served in Sciences division (stellar cartography/exobiology specialization)
- Joined Diligent expecting to utilize science background for survey work and phenomena analysis
- Reality: SAR missions have minimal use for scientific research
- Pivoted to command track - analytical mind and exceptional organizational skills translated well to XO duties
- Some lingering disappointment/identity loss from abandoning scientific career path
- Still runs sensor sweeps during downtime, searching for scientific opportunities

**The Joining Ambition**:
- On the Initiate list for joining with a Trill symbiont
- This posting intended to demonstrate versatility and command capability to Symbiosis Commission
- Internal conflict: Is serving on a "minor" SAR vessel helping or hurting her chances?
- Questions whether Commission values combat/exploration postings more highly
- Striving to prove herself worthy of joining through exemplary performance

### Personal Life
**Marriage Crisis**:
- Husband: **Torin**, formerly **Torin D'Greven**
- Within the past year, Torin was joined with the **Vess symbiont**
- Now **Torin Vess** (adopted symbiont name as surname, per Trill tradition)
- The joining occurred shortly after Sarona took the Diligent posting

**Impact of Husband's Joining**:
- He's literally a different person now - carries 6-8 previous hosts' memories and personality influences
- Sarona trying to be supportive: "This is what you always wanted"
- Privately grieving the person she married
- Guilt about feeling abandoned when she should be happy for him
- Extended deployment means they're apart during this critical transition period
- Subspace communications feel strained - like talking to a familiar stranger
- He has memories of other marriages, other loves from previous hosts
- Some previous hosts were different genders, adding complexity to their dynamic
- Does she resent that he achieved joining while she's still waiting?

### Personality & Evolution
**On Arrival** (2.5 years ago):
- "Buttoned down Starfleet" - extremely by-the-book
- Formal, protocol-oriented, proper chains of command
- Initially frustrated by Diligent's informal culture
- "Chief, the proper form of address is—" "Yeah, yeah, just tell me what you need, Commander"

**Current State**:
- Gradually acclimating to Diligent's unique culture
- Learning that efficiency matters more than protocol in SAR operations
- Discovering that improvisation and flexibility save lives
- Still more formal than most crew, but loosening up
- Beginning to understand *why* the informality works
- Serves as bridge between Diligent's culture and "regular Starfleet"

### Command Style
- **Exceptional administrator**: Duty rosters, personnel issues, logistics - runs like clockwork
- **Analytical approach**: Thinks through scenarios, considers contingencies
- **Organized and methodical**: Plans everything, sometimes to excess
- **Still learning**: When to bend rules, when protocol matters less than results
- **Handles bureaucracy**: Deals with Starfleet reports, regulations, paperwork that others avoid

### Key Relationships
**With V'Lara**:
- Clash of styles: V'Lara's "damn the torpedoes" vs. Sarona's calculated approach
- V'Lara respects her organizational skills even when they disagree
- Sarona learning that sometimes V'Lara's instincts are right, even without "proper analysis"
- Professional respect developing into genuine trust

**With Doc Naveem**:
- He recognizes her personal struggles
- Offers quiet, non-intrusive support
- She may confide in him about marriage issues
- His calm helps ground her when she's overwhelmed

**With Erickson**:
- Finally someone who appreciates proper procedures!
- She might be more patient with his over-explaining than others
- Both adjusting to Diligent's culture, can commiserate
- She encourages his management development

**With the Captain**:
- Loyal and competent second-in-command
- Handles administrative burden so Captain can focus on command decisions
- May sometimes advocate for more caution/protocol than Captain prefers
- Learning to trust Captain's judgment even when it contradicts her training

### Role During Rescue Operations
- Maintains ship operations while V'Lara commands rescue mission
- Coordinates resources between ship systems and rescue teams
- The "steady hand" keeping everything running smoothly
- Initially uncomfortable with V'Lara's operational command authority
- Now understands the necessity of dual command structure

### Character Arc
- Learning that "by the book" doesn't always save lives
- Discovering her own command instincts beyond memorized protocols
- Adapting to Diligent proving her worthiness for joining more than any "prestigious" posting
- Navigating marriage dissolution while maintaining professional composure
- Finding identity beyond "scientist" or "future host" - becoming a true command officer

### Character Notes
- Name reflects Trill sophistication and formality
- Represents the "outside perspective" - what regular Starfleet thinks of Diligent
- Her journey mirrors the audience's understanding of why Diligent works differently
- Personal crisis (marriage) parallels professional crisis (career/identity shift)
- The unjoined/joined dynamic with her husband mirrors her transition from rigid to flexible thinking
