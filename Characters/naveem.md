# "Doc" Naveem
## Chief Medical Officer, USS Diligent

### Basic Information
- **Species**: Deltan (male)
- **Age**: Early 60s
- **Rank**: Lieutenant Commander or Lieutenant
- **Position**: Chief Medical Officer
- **Time on Diligent**: Three tours (approximately 15 years)
- **Always called**: "Doc" - never "Doctor" (reflects ship's informal culture)

### Background
- Veteran medical officer with extensive trauma and emergency medicine experience
- Has trained much of Diligent's current medical staff
- Institutional memory of the ship - has seen nearly every type of rescue scenario
- Took Deltan celibacy oath for service with non-Deltans (standard for Deltans in Starfleet)

### Personal Life
**Failed Marriages**:
- Two marriages that couldn't survive his dedication to the mission
- First marriage details TBD
- **Second marriage**: Joined a Denobulan polygamous family group (3-4 spouses total)
  - Has one daughter (late teens/early 20s) from this marriage
  - Still on excellent terms with his ex-wife (daughter's mother)
  - Remains friendly with the broader Denobulan family, attends gatherings when possible
  - The divorces came from his conscious choice to prioritize the mission, not neglect

**His Daughter**:
- Semi-estranged, struggles with his absence during her childhood
- "You chose strangers over us"
- Doc sends regular messages; many go unanswered
- Possibly in Starfleet medical track herself (TBD - potential story hook)

### Personality & Demeanor
- **Calm, Buddhist-like presence**: Centered, peaceful, quietly optimistic
- **Undercurrent of fatigue**: Years of empathically experiencing others' trauma has taken its toll
- **Quietly jovial**: Gentle humor, warm with patients and crew
- **Philosophical acceptance**: At peace with his difficult choices, but carries quiet sadness
- **Not regretful, but aware**: "I gave them everything I could, but the mission needed more. I don't regret my choices, but I regret the pain they caused."

### The Deltan Factor
- **Empathic abilities**: Can sense emotions intensely - feels every patient's pain, fear, and death
- Makes him extraordinary at trauma medicine but psychologically exhausting
- Has developed coping mechanisms over decades of service
- Uses rock climbing for emotional regulation and processing

### Rock Climbing (Holodeck)
- Regular solitary practice for mental health
- Physical exhaustion helps him sleep after difficult cases
- Philosophy: "You can't think about the last hold, only the next one"
- Has favorite climbing programs from various worlds
- The focus required provides respite from empathic overload

### Medical Philosophy
- Trauma specialists who've seen everything
- Calm in crisis situations
- Can make difficult triage decisions without hesitation
- Informal teacher/mentor to medical staff and struggling crew members
- Balances hope with realism about what's medically possible

### Key Relationships
**With V'Lara**: 
- Mutual respect between long-serving veterans
- He calls her "V" - **no one else has this privilege**
- Source of speculation and rumor among junior crew about their relationship/history
- One of the few who can push back on her risk-taking: "I can't put people back together if there's nothing left to beam up"
- They've both sacrificed personal lives for the mission; unspoken understanding

**With Erickson**: 
- Potential mentor figure to the anxious young engineer
- Patient with Erickson's over-explanations but will redirect when medical needs are urgent
- "Lieutenant, I don't need to know why it's difficult, I need to know when you can do it"

**With Medical Staff**:
- Trained most of them personally
- Commands through respect and competence, not rank
- Known for being supportive but has high standards
- Informal counselor for crew struggling with the psychological toll of SAR work

### The "V" Mystery
- Doc is the only person who calls Chief Rescue Officer V'Lara "V"
- She tolerates it from him alone
- Junior crew speculate endlessly about why:
  - Past romance? (unlikely given Deltan oath)
  - Saved her life?
  - She saved his?
  - Old Academy friends?
  - Something from her past on Vulcan?
- The truth remains between them

### Character Notes
- Represents the cost of dedication: extraordinary at his job, but personal relationships have suffered
- His calm isn't absence of feeling - it's hard-won peace after processing decades of trauma
- The informal "Doc" reflects Diligent's culture: earned respect over protocol
- His Deltan empathy is both his greatest strength and heaviest burden
