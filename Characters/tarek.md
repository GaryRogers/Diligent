# Senior Chief Petty Officer Tarek Jorin
## SAR Operations Chief, USS Diligent

### Basic Information
- **Species**: Bajoran
- **Age**: Early 40s
- **Rank**: Senior Chief Petty Officer (eligible for Master Chief, declines promotion)
- **Position**: SAR Operations Chief - V'Lara's right hand, primary field operator
- **Time on Diligent**: Approximately 5-7 years working with V'Lara
- **Names used**: 
  - "Senior Chief Tarek" or "Chief Tarek" (most crew)
  - "Jori" (V'Lara only - shows close working relationship)

### Background
**Early Life**:
- Born during or shortly after Bajoran Occupation
- Parents died during the occupation (circumstances: Cardassian reprisal/work camp/resistance activity - details TBD)
- Raised by extended family with support from **Vedek Franelis Tarek** (Aunt Fran)
- Grew up seeing Starfleet as liberators and helpers
- This shaped his dedication to rescue work - paying forward what his people received

**Career Path**:
- Enlisted in Starfleet as **Engineering Crewman** right out of school
- Good with hands, mechanical aptitude, natural problem-solver
- Mid-20s during Dominion War (2373-2375) - saw heavy combat
- During the war chaos, ended up doing rescue and recovery operations
- A commander (possibly deceased) recognized his talents: "You're wasted fixing conduits. You should be saving lives."
- Transferred to SAR operations specialty
- Never looked back - found his true calling
- 15-20 years as SAR specialist
- Joined Diligent crew approximately 5-7 years ago

### The Promotion Question
- Fully qualified and eligible for Master Chief Petty Officer
- **Actively declines promotion**
- Master Chief would mean administration, management, desk work
- "I'll take the desk when my knees give out, not before"
- Wants to remain hands-on field operator as long as physically capable
- Estimates 3-5 more years of active operations realistically possible
- Mirrors V'Lara's own refusal of promotion to Commander
- Currently mentoring next generation of SAR operators to eventually replace him

### Personality & Traits
**In Crisis**:
- **Calm under pressure**: The worse things get, the more focused he becomes
- **Doesn't hesitate**: War taught him that hesitation kills
- **Balanced risk assessment**: Also learned that recklessness kills
- **Practical, not philosophical**: Doesn't overthink, acts on training and instinct
- **Steady hands, steady voice**: Reassuring presence in chaos

**Daily Demeanor**:
- **Gruff exterior, soft heart**: Cares deeply, shows it through actions not words
- **Protective of his team**: Won't risk them unnecessarily, but will go himself
- **Mentor**: Teaches through doing, leads by example
- **Straightforward**: Says what he means, means what he says
- **Working-class sensibility**: Practical, unpretentious, values competence over rank

**Bajoran Identity**:
- Still has faith in the Prophets (wears traditional earring)
- Not vocally religious, but it grounds him
- "The Prophets guide, but I do the work"
- Proud of Bajoran heritage but doesn't make it his whole identity
- SAR work as spiritual calling - honoring those who couldn't be saved

### Skills & Expertise
**Field Operations**:
- Rope work and climbing (rappelling into disaster sites)
- Zero-gravity rescue operations
- Environmental suit work (toxic atmospheres, vacuum, extreme conditions)
- Structural damage assessment (engineering background)
- Equipment jury-rigging in the field
- Triage and medical stabilization basics
- Pattern enhancer deployment for emergency transport

**Engineering Knowledge**:
- Can read how structures will fail
- Knows how systems break under stress
- Can improvise solutions with limited resources
- Understands ship systems from his crewman days

**Leadership**:
- Coordinates multi-person rescue teams
- Makes rapid tactical decisions
- Trains junior SAR operators
- Interfaces between V'Lara's strategy and ground-level execution

### The Dominion War Shadow
- Mid-20s during the war - saw heavy action, lost shipmates
- Probably lost his original ship
- Carries survivor's guilt but channels it into saving others
- Has PTSD but functional - work is his therapy
- Understands Harlow's desire to prove herself but knows she's untempered
- Doesn't romanticize combat like Harlow does - knows the real cost

### Key Relationships
**With V'Lara**:
- Works directly under her during rescue operations
- 5-7 years together - they're in perfect sync
- He anticipates her needs, she trusts his judgment implicitly
- One of the few who can push back: "Chief, that's not survivable"
- She calls him "Jori" - nobody else gets this privilege
- Mutual respect between professionals who've both refused advancement
- He makes her plans work on the ground; she gives him the authority to do it

**With Doc Naveem**:
- Fellow veteran who understands the weight of the work
- Medical and SAR coordinate constantly during operations
- Probably share a drink when missions go badly
- Doc patches up Jori's team (and Jori himself) regularly
- Mutual respect and quiet friendship

**With Harlow**:
- Sees her eagerness and inexperience clearly
- Reminds him of green troops during the war
- Patient but firm - won't let her get herself or others killed
- "Lieutenant, secure the perimeter means stay PUT"
- Could be valuable mentor if she listens
- Probably reports concerns to V'Lara when Harlow takes unnecessary risks

**With Erickson**:
- Works closely during technical rescues
- Needs engineering support for structural assessments, power requirements
- Appreciates Erickson's technical knowledge
- Sometimes frustrated by over-explanation when time is critical
- "Lieutenant, I don't need the theory, I need that bulkhead stabilized NOW"

**With D'Greven**:
- Coordinates logistics and resource allocation through her
- Professional relationship
- She ensures he has what he needs; he makes efficient use of it
- Probably appreciates her organizational skills

**With SAR Team**:
- Leads by example, first in/last out mentality
- Protective but realistic about dangers
- Trains constantly - "We drill so we don't have to think"
- Known for bringing his people home
- They'd follow him anywhere

### Family
**Vedek Franelis Tarek ("Aunt Fran")**:
- Jorin's aunt (paternal or maternal - TBD)
- Member of the Vedek Assembly
- "Uncontroversial" - doesn't court public attention
- Political operator: builds coalitions, trades favors, behind-the-scenes power
- Sharp, pragmatic, uses religious position for practical outcomes
- Stepped in after Jorin's parents died during occupation
- Didn't raise him full-time but was his emotional anchor
- They communicate regularly via subspace
- She updates him on Bajoran politics; he tells her about rescues
- Proud he's in Starfleet but wishes he'd visit more
- "You have a Vedek aunt and you're wasting it being a grease monkey in space"
- Thinks he should leverage his position more politically (he refuses)

### Personal Philosophy
- **"The Prophets guide, but I do the work"**: Faith and action in balance
- **"We drill so we don't have to think"**: Training saves lives
- **"First in, last out"**: Leader's responsibility
- **"Nobody saved my parents. I save everyone I can."**: Core motivation
- Values competence over rank, action over words
- Believes in earning respect through deed, not demanding it through position
