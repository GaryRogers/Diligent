# Episode 1.1: "First Protocol"

## Episode Overview
Captain Sev arrives aboard the USS Diligent. Expecting a smoothly-run Starfleet vessel, they find a veteran crew operating with casual efficiency. The first rescue operation immediately tests Sev's by-the-book protocols against V'Lara's operational pragmatism.

**POV**: V'Lara

**Theme**: Protocol vs. Reality

---

## Act Structure

### Act One: Arrival & Assessment

#### Scene 1: Handover Ceremony at Space Dock
- **Location**: Space Dock airlock/docking area
- **Characters**: Sev, outgoing Captain, V'Lara, Tarek, D'Greven, Erickson, Harlow, assorted crew
- **Tone**: Formal transition interrupted by urgency
- **Key Moments**:
  - Sev arrives for formal handover with outgoing Captain
  - Ceremony is polite, brief—not a prestigious posting
  - Outgoing Captain shakes hands: "She's a good ship. The crew is solid."
  - Before full briefing can begin, alert comes through
  - Distress call: cargo vessel emergency situation developing
  - Outgoing Captain looks at V'Lara: "It's yours, Captain. Good luck."
  - Sev gets immediate baptism into SAR operations—no grace period
  - First impression: organized chaos masquerading as efficiency (crew rushing to stations, crew shouting coordinates, everyone moving with practiced urgency)

#### Scene 2: Meet the Chief Rescue Officer (Corridor/Bridge)
- **Location**: Moving from docking area to bridge during emergency mobilization
- **Characters**: Sev, V'Lara, brief crew interactions
- **Tone**: Professional but tense, practical amid chaos
- **Key Moments**:
  - V'Lara materialize at Sev's side as crew rushes past
  - Quick introduction while moving: "V'Lara, Chief Rescue Officer. Welcome to Diligent, Captain."
  - Sev trying to understand the situation while crew swarms around them
  - V'Lara already running scenario, already thinking about team deployment
  - Sev's instinct: "Shouldn't we complete the handover briefing? Understand protocols?"
  - V'Lara's response: "We will. After. Right now people need us. Come."
  - Establish the tension: V'Lara's operational independence vs. Sev's need for procedure

#### Scene 3: Bridge - Taking the Conn
- **Location**: Bridge (in motion/action)
- **Characters**: Sev, V'Lara, Tarek, D'Greven, Erickson, Harlow, crew
- **Tone**: Controlled urgency, crew confidence
- **Key Moments**:
  - Sev takes captain's chair for the first time during actual crisis
  - Crew doesn't pause for formal introduction—they're already working
  - D'Greven rattling off logistics
  - Erickson coordinating power systems
  - Harlow running security protocols
  - Tarek gathering SAR team prep data
  - V'Lara presenting tactical situation
  - Sev processing: no ceremony, no briefing, just action
  - Observe the casual competence: crew knowing their roles, no hesitation

---

### Act Two: First Crisis & Conflict

#### Scene 4: Distress Call
- **Location**: Bridge
- **Characters**: Sev, V'Lara, Tarek, D'Greven, Erickson, Harlow
- **Tone**: Urgency vs. procedure
- **Key Moments**:
  - Distress call: cargo vessel, unknown emergency
  - V'Lara immediate response: "Launch SAR teams. Get to warp."
  - Sev immediate response: "Full pre-flight checklist. Risk assessment. Proper launch sequence."
  - V'Lara exchanges look with Tarek: *Here we go again*
  - The delay as Sev insists on procedure

#### Scene 5: The Launch Delay
- **Location**: Bridge (tension building)
- **Characters**: Sev, V'Lara, engineering team coordinating
- **Tone**: Mounting frustration
- **Key Moments**:
  - Systems checks taking longer than crew expects
  - Real-time emergency updates: situation deteriorating
  - V'Lara's jaw tightens, but she defers to Captain's authority
  - Erickson rushing through systems checks
  - The human cost of delay becoming visible in transmissions

#### Scene 6: En Route
- **Location**: Bridge
- **Characters**: Sev, V'Lara, Tarek, others maintaining operational status
- **Tone**: Professional but underlying tension
- **Key Moments**:
  - Sev asking logical questions; V'Lara providing tactical information
  - Tarek coordinating SAR team prep
  - Situation escalating at cargo vessel
  - Final approach: "We're still going to make it. Barely."

---

### Act Three: The Rescue & Reckoning

#### Scene 7: Warp Core Leak Discovery
- **Location**: Bridge, sensors/tactical
- **Characters**: Sev, V'Lara, Erickson, Harlow, crew
- **Tone**: New problem, shifting tactics
- **Key Moments**:
  - Erickson: "Captain, we're detecting warp core instability on the freighter."
  - Harlow: "Radiation signature increasing. Transporters are compromised."
  - V'Lara assessing: transporters are down. Full evacuation requires shuttle extraction.
  - Sev: "How long until the core fails?"
  - Erickson: "Unknown. Could be hours. Could be minutes."
  - V'Lara immediately shifting to contingency: shuttle launch
  - Complication raises stakes—more people at risk

#### Scene 8: Preparing the Rescue Shuttle
- **Location**: Flight bay
- **Characters**: Tarek, SAR team members, flight crew, Erickson's engineers
- **Tone**: Precise, professional, practiced efficiency
- **Key Moments**:
  - Tarek coordinating SAR team gear and assignment
  - Each team member knows their role—no confusion, no hesitation
  - Flight crew running through launch checklist with methodical precision
  - Tarek to team: "Radiation suit protocols. We're hot and we're in and out. Nobody overextends."
  - Engineers checking shuttle systems with practiced calm
  - Equipment placement efficient, everything accounted for
  - V'Lara's voice over comms: "Shuttle One, what's your status?"
  - Tarek: "Team is ready. Shuttle is go. Awaiting launch clearance."
  - This scene shows the crew at their best—professional, focused, competent

#### Scene 9: Bridge - Launch Sequence
- **Location**: Bridge
- **Characters**: Sev, V'Lara, D'Greven, Erickson, tactical crew
- **Tone**: Coordinated urgency
- **Key Moments**:
  - V'Lara to Sev: "Launch sequence ready when you give the order, Captain."
  - Sev hesitates—this is the decision point
  - V'Lara's steady presence: not pushing, just waiting
  - Sev realizes: people are dying right now. No time for extended risk assessment.
  - Sev: "Launch the shuttle."
  - D'Greven: "Shuttle One, you are clear for launch."
  - Precise coordination between bridge and shuttle as it launches
  - No wasted motion, no unnecessary chatter
  - Professional competence on display

#### Scene 10: On-Site Rescue Operations
- **Location**: Freighter interior (shuttle team perspective) and bridge (V'Lara coordinating)
- **Characters**: Tarek leading shuttle team, V'Lara directing from bridge, freighter crew in distress
- **Tone**: Dangerous, controlled, race against time
- **Key Moments**:
  - Shuttle docks with freighter despite radiation warnings
  - Tarek and team move with practiced precision through corridors
  - Radiation suits protecting but limiting movement
  - Crew members guided toward airlock by SAR team
  - V'Lara on comms: "Core temperature rising. You've got maybe eight minutes."
  - Tarek: "Copy. Loading civilians now."
  - Team making rapid assessments: who can move, who needs help, who's injured
  - Freighter crew responding to Tarek's directions with trust—they know the drill
  - Multiple shuttle runs if necessary, or one loaded shuttle with careful coordination
  - One person in lower decks: can't reach them before core failure
  - Tarek has to make the call: leave them or risk entire team and full shuttle
  - Decision: "We can't reach them. We're pulling out."
  - Anguish visible but professionalism maintained

### Act Four: Protocols Established

#### Scene 10: Bridge Briefing
- **Location**: Briefing room
- **Characters**: Sev, department heads (V'Lara, D'Greven, Erickson, Doc, Harlow)
- **Tone**: Formal, establishing new order
- **Key Moments**:
  - Sev outlines new protocols for all rescue operations
  - Mandatory pre-flight checklist (longer than current practice)
  - Risk assessment requirements
  - Chain-of-command clarification
  - Formal approval process before launch
  - Crew exchanges subtle glances

#### Scene 11: Crew Reactions (Interspersed)
- **Location**: Various
- **Characters**: Different crew members reacting to new protocols
- **Tone**: Resignation, frustration, observation
- **Key Moments**:
  - Engineering team sighing about longer checklist
  - D'Greven noting procedures in her log
  - Tarek quietly to V'Lara: "Going to be a long tour."
  - Doc observing without judgment
  - Harlow trying to be enthusiastic about "proper procedure"

#### Scene 12: V'Lara's Decision
- **Location**: Ready Room or private space
- **Characters**: V'Lara (alone or with Tarek)
- **Tone**: Assessment, patience, observation
- **Key Moments**:
  - V'Lara's internal monologue: evaluate, wait, observe
  - "He cared about the casualty. That's something."
  - "But he doesn't understand why we do what we do."
  - Decision: don't fight yet. Observe. See if he can learn.
  - Or if she'll spend the entire tour at odds with him

## Key Moments Summary

1. **Sev's First Impression**: Protocol-focused Captain meets casual rescue crew
2. **The Launch Delay**: V'Lara defers to authority despite disagreement
3. **The Casualty**: One person dies due to arrival delay
4. **Sev's Rationalization**: Blames insufficient preparation, not procedure
5. **V'Lara's Realization**: Fundamental philosophical difference
6. **New Protocols Established**: Sev implements stricter procedures
7. **Crew Reactions**: Mix of resignation and curiosity
8. **V'Lara's Decision**: Wait and observe rather than fight

## Character Arcs This Episode

**Sev**:
- Arrives confident in their command philosophy
- Encounters reality that doesn't match expectations
- Experiences their first command decision
- Defends their choice through logic
- Unaware of how their decision affected crew

**V'Lara**:
- Assesses new Captain with skepticism
- Respects the authority hierarchy despite disagreement
- Recognizes a fundamental values conflict
- Makes strategic decision to observe rather than rebel
- Plants seeds of internal conflict for later episodes

**Supporting Characters**:
- **Tarek**: Ally to V'Lara, wary of new protocols
- **Doc**: Observing, recognizing patterns
- **Erickson**: Concerned about longer procedures affecting response time
- **D'Greven**: Adapting, documenting, neutral
- **Harlow**: Eager to prove herself with new procedures

---

## Thematic Elements

- **Protocol vs. Reality**: Procedure isn't always sufficient
- **Authority vs. Expertise**: Who decides when procedures are too restrictive?
- **The Cost of Caution**: What is lost when we prioritize protocol over action?
- **First Impressions**: Both Sev and V'Lara forming judgments that will evolve
